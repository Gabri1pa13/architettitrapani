TECH & LUXURY

Control your Sicilian Villa from an App

You live in London or New York. Your villa is in Trapani. How do you know if the humidity is rising or if the gardener actually showed up? The answer is Domotics.

The "Welcome Mode"

Imagine landing at Palermo Airport. You open your phone, press "Arrival", and your villa wakes up: the AC turns on to reach 22°C, the garden lights illuminate the driveway, and the shutters open. This is standard in our luxury renovations.

Security & Maintenance

A smart home isn't just a toy; it's an investment guardian.

  • Water leak sensors: Shut off the main valve automatically if a leak is detected while you are away.
  • Humidity control: Automatically turn on dehumidifiers if the Sicilian winter gets too damp, protecting your furniture and art.
  • Video access: Check the perimeter cameras instantly.
