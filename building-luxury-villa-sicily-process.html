Building from Scratch: Your Custom Villa

Renovating is romantic, but building new allows for perfection. Large windows, perfect insulation, and zero compromise.

Finding a plot of land with "buildability" (indice di edificabilità) in Trapani or Selinunte is difficult but possible. We specialize in contemporary villas that blend into the landscape.

The Timeline (Realistic)

  • Design & Permits (6-9 Months): Italian bureaucracy is slow. We need approval from the Comune, Genio Civile (Seismic), and Soprintendenza (Landscape).
  • Construction (12-14 Months): For a high-end villa with pool.

Why build new?

Energy efficiency. A new build by Studio 4e is an NZEB (Near Zero Energy Building). With solar panels and modern insulation, your running costs for AC and heating will be almost zero.
