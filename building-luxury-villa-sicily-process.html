Building from Scratch: Your Custom Villa

Renovating is romantic, but building new allows for perfection. Large windows, perfect insulation, and zero compromise.

Finding a plot of land with "buildability" (indice di edificabilità) in Trapani or Selinunte is difficult but possible. We specialize in contemporary villas that blend into the landscape.

The Timeline (Realistic)

  • Design & Permits (6-9 Months): Italian bureaucracy is slow. We need approval from the Comune, Genio Civile (Seismic), and Soprintendenza (Landscape).
  • Construction (12-14 Months): For a high-end villa with pool.

Why build new?

Energy efficiency. A new build by Studio 4e is an NZEB (Near Zero Energy Building). With solar panels and modern insulation, your running costs for AC and heating will be almost zero.

For luxury villa projects on the Ionian coast, including Taormina and the Messina area, visit Architetti Taormina where we specialize in high-end residential design with landscape constraints.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a villa in Sicily?

Total timeline: approximately 18–23 months. The permit phase takes 6–9 months (Comune, Genio Civile, Soprintendenza), followed by 12–14 months of construction for a high-end villa with pool. Italian bureaucracy is the main variable — starting with an experienced local architect keeps timelines predictable.

Can I build a new house in a protected area in Sicily?

In protected landscape zones (zona vincolata), new construction is heavily restricted. However, renovation and expansion of existing structures is often possible with Soprintendenza approval. New builds in unprotected agricultural or residential zones are subject to the local PRG and the land's indice di edificabilità.

What does NZEB mean for a villa in Sicily?

NZEB (Near Zero Energy Building) is the EU standard for all new construction since 2021. An NZEB villa in Sicily combines high-performance insulation, triple-glazed windows, and solar panels to bring heating and cooling costs near-zero. In Sicily's climate (300+ sunny days/year), new NZEB villas are both legally required and exceptionally efficient.
