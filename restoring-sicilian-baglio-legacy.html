HERITAGE & LEGACY

The Baglio: More than a house, a piece of History

The "Baglio" is the architectural soul of Western Sicily. A fortified courtyard building where life happened. Owning one is a responsibility.

The Philosophy of "Conservative Restoration"

When we approach a Baglio (like our award-winning project at Donna Franca), our rule is: Listen to the building. We clean the original tuff stone, we restore the "basole" (stone floors), and we keep the scars of time.

Modern Comfort, Invisible Tech

The challenge is to make a 300-year-old stone fortress comfortable in 2026.

  • Underfloor heating: Hidden under reclaimed stone tiles.
  • Thermal insulation: Applied on the roof, never covering the beautiful stone walls.
  • Lighting: Minimalist fixtures that highlight the arches without stealing the show.
