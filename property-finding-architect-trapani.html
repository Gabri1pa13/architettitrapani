EXCLUSIVE SERVICES

Property Finding: We are on YOUR side

Real estate agents work for the seller (mostly). We work exclusively for you, the buyer. There is a huge difference.

Buying a property in Sicily from abroad is risky. Photos are edited, wide-angle lenses make rooms look huge, and nobody mentions the damp patch on the ceiling or the illegal veranda.

How our Scout Service works

  • Briefing: You tell us your dream (e.g., "Sea view, historic stone, max €200k").
  • Scouting: We use our local network to find properties (even off-market ones) that match.
  • The "Cruel" Inspection: We visit the shortlist. We don't look at the view; we look at the cracks, the roof, the humidity. We destroy the romance to protect your wallet.
  • Negotiation: We give you the *real* renovation costs so you can negotiate the asking price down.
