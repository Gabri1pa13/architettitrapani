LOCATION BATTLE

Why Smart Investors are Choosing Trapani over Palermo

Palermo is beautiful, vibrant, and culturally rich. But it is also chaotic, loud, and traffic-congested. Trapani offers the "Slow Coast" lifestyle.

For international buyers looking for a holiday home or a retirement retreat, Western Sicily (The Trapani Province) is winning the comparison. Here is why.

1. The Ocean is Right There

In Palermo, you often have to drive 30 minutes to find a clean beach (Mondello is crowded). In Trapani, Marsala, or Selinunte, the sea is part of daily life. The water quality is superior.

2. Easy Access (Birgi Airport)

Trapani-Birgi Airport (TPS) is small, stress-free, and connects directly to many European capitals. You can land and be at your villa in 20 minutes. No traffic jams.

3. Better Value for Money

With €150,000 in Palermo, you buy an apartment that needs renovation in a noisy street. In the Trapani province, for the same price, you can find a small independent house with a garden or sea view.
