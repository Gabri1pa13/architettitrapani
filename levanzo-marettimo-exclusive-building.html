EXCLUSIVE LOCATIONS

The Wild Islands: Levanzo & Marettimo

Favignana is beautiful, but for those seeking absolute silence and zero cars, Levanzo and Marettimo are the ultimate luxury. Building here is an adventure.

Extreme Logistics

There are no hardware stores on Marettimo. Every bag of cement, every tile, every faucet must arrive by ship.
On Levanzo, there are no cars. Materials are transported by specialized electric mules or, for high-end projects, by helicopter. We manage this complex supply chain so you don't have to.

Water Independence

Water is gold on these islands. Our projects incorporate sophisticated rainwater collection systems (underground cisterns restoration) and desalination units, ensuring your villa is self-sufficient even in August.
